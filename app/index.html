Privacy & Terms

Review how your data is handled and the terms of service.

Data Storage: All financial data is stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB). No data is sent to external servers except for:

  • eBay API sync (if enabled) - sends only order data to your own eBay account
  • Plaid Link (if enabled) - tokens exchanged with Plaid for account aggregation
  • Price quotes - public ticker symbols to Yahoo Finance

No Tracking: No analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use an API integration.
