Every retirement plan assumes nothing goes wrong. fire's 🌪️ Chaos mode rolls real life onto your projection: surgeries, a dog's ACL, kids, an aging parent, and the good stuff too, like an inherited house. Every marker tells you what happened and what it cost. Tick the coverage you have (pet insurance, an HSA, an umbrella policy) and it shows what that saves. Try it live at lifefire.netlify.app · github.com/nitsuah/fire
